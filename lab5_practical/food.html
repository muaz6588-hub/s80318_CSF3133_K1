About Festival

Taste of Terengganu brings together the best local food stalls for three days of eating, music and culture by the river.Visitor can try famous dishes such as nasi dagang, keropok lekor and laksam,prepared by home cooks and small bussinesses from all over the state.

The fastival also support entrepreneurs and students.Cookingf demonstration are held every evening, and stalls follow the festival's green guideline by using or biodegradable packing.

Free entry fo all UMT studens-show your matric card at the entrance.
