About Us

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a premier public university in Malaysia focused on marine science, aquatic resources, and oceanography. Established with a strong commitment to academic and research excellence, UMT plays a pivotal role in exploring and conserving aquatic environments and sustainable coastal development.

Located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, the university provides a conducive and vibrant learning environment equipped with state-of-the-art research facilities, specialized marine vessels, and dedicated academic staff nurturing future leaders and researchers.
